Quinoa Buddha Bowl Shopping Guide (Serves 2)

Ingredients and Estimated Costs:

1. Grains:
   - Quinoa (1 cup): $1.50

2. Legumes:
   - Chickpeas (1 can): $0.80

3. Vegetables (Produce):
   - Broccoli (1 head): $2.00
   - Baby Spinach (3 cups): $2.00
   - Bell Pepper (1 medium): $0.80
   - Avocado (1): $1.20
   - Red Onion (1): $0.50

4. Pantry items:
   - Tahini (2 tablespoons): $0.80
   - Lemon (1): $0.40
   - Garlic (2 cloves): $0.20
   - Salt: $0.00 (on hand)
   - Olive Oil (1 tablespoon): $0.25
   - Pepper: $0.00 (on hand)
   - Cumin (1 teaspoon): $0.20

Total Estimated Cost: $9.95

Budget Analysis:
The estimated total cost of $9.95 is well within the $20.00 budget for two servings. This leaves room for additional seasonings, sauces, or a small protein addition if desired.

Money-Saving Tips:

1. Buy ingredients in bulk, such as quinoa, chickpeas, and spices, to reduce per-unit costs, especially if you plan to make multiple Buddha bowls.

2. Substitute expensive ingredients with cheaper alternatives:
   - Replace tahini with peanut butter for a similar flavor at a lower cost.
   - Use a small amount of lemon juice if a whole lemon seems wasteful.
   - Choose seasonal produce for better prices on vegetables like bell peppers and broccoli.

3. Purchase ingredients on sale or with coupons to further reduce costs.

4. Consider buying a small quantity of cumin if you don't already have it, as it's a versatile spice that can be used in many other dishes.

5. Take advantage of store brands, which often offer similar quality at lower prices compared to name brands.

The Quinoa Buddha Bowl recipe is a nutritious and satisfying meal that meets dietary restrictions (vegetarian, high protein) while staying well within the $20 budget. By following the money-saving tips, you can further stretch your grocery budget and enjoy a delicious, healthy meal.
